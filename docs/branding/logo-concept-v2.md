## Prompt

Design a sophisticated original corporate identity for Zyvello. This must look
like a carefully resolved identity from a top independent brand design studio,
not an AI startup logo template. Show ONE finished horizontal logo on a clean
warm ivory background, with much generous negative space. Exact wordmark text:
Zyvello. Beside it create an ORIGINAL ABSTRACT EMBLEM called the 'open fold': a
compact near-square sculptural silhouette built from ONLY TWO thick interlocking
rounded angular shapes, with a single precise off-center negative-space aperture
connecting them. The emblem suggests a continuous exchange or a relationship
opening into opportunity, only very subtly suggesting a hidden Z through negative
space. It must NOT look like a literal letter Z, a lightning bolt, a slashed
letter, an envelope, a chain-link icon, infinity loop, circular arrows, hexagon,
star, sparkle, generic AI knot, or a stack of lines. Explore asymmetric balance:
one solid rounded corner and one deliberate square-cut opening. Extremely simple,
ownable, memorable, strong at 16 pixels, monolithic silhouette. Flat one-color deep
charcoal #171D24 for BOTH mark and wordmark; no gradients and no lime slash.
Wordmark should be custom-drawn humanist geometric sans serif with restrained
personality, a distinctive lowercase y and subtly squared counters, correct
spelling Zyvello, optically spaced, medium weight. Symbol about the height of the
wordmark capital Z plus its descender area; modest elegant gap. Precision
vector-like edges, excellent proportions, premium enterprise software company,
calm and authoritative. No decorative backgrounds, no slogans, no explanation
text, no identity-board layouts, no presentation mockups, no shadows, no texture,
no 3D. Only symbol and wordmark, presented beautifully at high resolution. Avoid
reproducing any existing company trademark.
